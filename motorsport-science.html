Corner Radius & Lateral g

0.71 g

a = v² / r. Steady circular motion on a level surface; computed lateral acceleration is a demand, not available grip.

Braking Kinetic Energy

385.8 kJ

E = ½mv². Translational kinetic energy available to dissipate or recover from this speed to rest, excluding rotating components and other losses.

Combined Tire Force

80% lateral capacity remains

Illustrative friction circle: lateral share = √(1 − braking share²). Real tire force envelopes depend on load, slip, surface and tire construction.
