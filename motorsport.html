BUTTONWILLOW · APRIL 2026

From instruction to independent analysis.

I started with classroom instruction and coaching in the car, then progressed to driving independently. I learned braking points and racing lines through practice, but I also began to notice how difficult it was to judge a lap by feel alone.

During training, I began to realize that top speed alone did not determine my lap time. Corner speed, especially exit speed, mattered. I also noticed that reaching my usual upshift RPM sooner after a corner could help me accelerate along the following straight. This was a driving observation, not a measured test of an earlier shift point.

After driving, I explored the AiM data provided by Hicks Motorsports. Cooper Hicks had coached me on the uphill U-turn line; AI tools later helped visualize the paths and their speed and timing values. I proposed the question of how the line related to exit speed. The two selected April 20 laps differ by 7.401 seconds, but I still need a controlled comparison to test that relationship.
