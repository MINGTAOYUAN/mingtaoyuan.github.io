How the explorer works

Thirty original introductory notes connect motorsport physics, vehicle energy, electric propulsion and battery safety. Deterministic keyword ranking selects up to four related entries. The backend repeats retrieval using its own copy of the library, then checks the structure and citation IDs of any generated answer. Citation checks cannot prove that every sentence is correct.

Sources are public references or clearly labelled original method notes and simplified models. Source organizations have not endorsed these explanations. One additional chemistry comparison is withheld pending direct source review. No private RWTH course slides or handouts are indexed, uploaded or reproduced.

Knowledge search keeps working when AI is unavailable or its allowance is reached. The Data Explorer separately compares actual telemetry. Read the Motorsport Science models or the automotive learning cards for further context.
