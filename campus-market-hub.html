STUDENT INITIATIVE · IN DEVELOPMENT

Campus
Market Hub.

Working with fellow students on an exchange platform for the Fryeburg Academy dorm community.

Current stage: preparing for a limited dorm-student pilot. The pilot has not launched.

THE IDEA

Useful items.
New owners.

The project aims to help dorm students find new owners for useful items they no longer need. Students would use the website to discover items and arrange an exchange, then inspect and exchange them in person.

The first version focuses on listings and communication. Payments would take place offline.

MY ROLE / WORKING WITH OTHERS

From a website to a school proposal.

I am working with two fellow students on how the platform could operate at school. I presented the proposal to school leaders and am helping turn their feedback into a plan for a small pilot.

The prototype uses AI-assisted development. My work on the project includes presenting the idea, discussing how students could use it, and planning student participation and responsibilities.

WHAT THE FEEDBACK CHANGED

A smaller first step.

The school supported continued development and asked that the pilot include only dorm students. The planning now connects website features with the people who would verify membership, review listings and help with problems.

Who can trade?

The pilot plan limits trading membership to verified dorm students. Student dorm leaders are the proposed first contact for confirming membership.

What can be listed?

School feedback called for ownership declarations, risk acknowledgement and a way to remove harmful content. These requirements guide the listing and moderation process.

Who helps when something goes wrong?

The proposed structure gives students day-to-day operating responsibilities, with school staff providing guidance and oversight and helping when disputes need intervention.

NEXT STEP

Test the process with people.

The immediate plan is a small internal test before extending access to dorm students. It would check registration, dorm verification, listing review and communication, alongside the practical arrangements for an in-person exchange.

Before the pilot opens, the team still needs to agree on responsibilities, exchange arrangements and how students can ask for help with the school. These are planning goals; participation and transaction results will be added after there is real use to report.
