Project background & what I am learning

I first wanted to explore questions raised by my driving experience: where did time change, and what could the recorded data explain? Topics I encountered at RWTH Aachen widened those questions to vehicle dynamics, propulsion and batteries.

This project uses AI-assisted development to connect data comparison with source-linked learning notes. I am using it to explore how a measured pattern, a physics explanation and a proposed test differ. AI helps develop the tools and generate explanations; those explanations still need to be checked against the data and sources.

The knowledge search and models can be explored without uploading telemetry. The Data Explorer below starts with your own files. The tools remain learning prototypes, and AI answers may be wrong.
