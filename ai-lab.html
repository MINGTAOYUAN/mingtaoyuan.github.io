AI LAB grew out of my earlier motorsport data project. The Full Report documents that original investigation and engineering reasoning. I later developed AI LAB as an AI-assisted tool for faster, more interactive data exploration.

Upload telemetry, compare basic traces, then ask where time may be lost and what to test next. AI answers are available in the private pilot with an access token.
