01 / THE QUESTION

What can the trace actually tell me?

I selected a complete lap from the 9:14 AM session and another from the 2:12 PM session on April 20, 2026. The AiM beacon markers give the lap times above. I compared GPS speed and elapsed time against normalized lap distance to see where the gap opened.

These laps come from separate sessions. The comparison documents a change, but by itself does not prove which driver input caused it.

04 / INTERPRETATION

From observation to hypothesis.

The faster lap is not explained by a single top speed. The traces show where speed and accumulated time differ throughout the lap. I would use those regions to review synchronized video, braking points, and corner exits before claiming a cause.

Next test: choose one repeatable corner, record the entry speed, minimum speed, and exit speed over several laps, then change one driving input at a time. Compare results under similar track conditions.

Analysis uses AiM exports at 20 Hz; distance is integrated from GPS speed. No brake pressure or throttle-position channels were supplied, so those inputs cannot be measured directly here.
